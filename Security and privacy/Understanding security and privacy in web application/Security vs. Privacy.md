# Security vs. Privacy

## Quick Summary

| | **Security** | **Privacy** |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Protect data from hackers and damage | Control how data is collected and used |
| **Analogy** | A lock on your front door | A curtain on your window |
| **Focus** | Technical defenses | Legal rights and policies |
| **Handled by** | Security teams | Privacy teams |
| **Examples** | Firewalls, encryption, passwords, antivirus | GDPR, consent, data-sharing rules |

---

## What Is Security?

Security protects data from **unauthorized access, theft, and malicious damage**. It builds a strong digital wall around your information.

**Analogy:** Security is a heavy lock on your front door. It keeps intruders out.

Security focuses on three things:

- **Confidentiality**: only the right people can see the data
- **Integrity**: the data is not altered or corrupted
- **Availability**: systems and data are accessible when needed

**Common tools:**

- Network firewalls
- Strong passwords
- Data encryption
- Antivirus software

> **Key point:** Security does not care what the data *means*. It only keeps the data safe from bad actors. Without it, stored data is open to immediate theft.

---

## What Is Privacy?

Privacy controls **how organizations collect, store, use, and share your personal data**. It gives you the power to decide who sees your information.

**Analogy:** Privacy is a window curtain. Even with a strong lock, people can see inside if the curtain is wide open.

Privacy decides whether a company can:

- Sell your browsing history
- Track your GPS location
- Share your email address with third-party advertisers

Laws such as the **GDPR** (European Union) strictly regulate data privacy. These laws will be covered in a future lecture.

---

## How Security and Privacy Relate

They are different concepts, but they depend on each other.

### Security without privacy: possible

A smartphone maker stores your location data in a highly secure cloud database. Hackers cannot get in.

But the company sells that data to advertisers without your permission.

- Security: works perfectly
- Privacy: none

### Privacy without security: impossible

A medical clinic promises never to share your health records. But its local servers are not secured, so a hacker steals the records.

- Promise of privacy: made
- Security: missing
- Result: privacy is destroyed

> **Conclusion:** Strong security is the **foundation** for effective privacy.

---

## Understanding the Difference

- **Security** = the technical defense against cyberattacks
- **Privacy** = the legal rights and policies on how data is used

Security teams build defenses against active attacks. Privacy teams make sure the organization follows privacy laws and respects user choices.

### Remember

> **Security locks the front door. Privacy draws the window blinds.**
> Together, they create a safe, trustworthy environment for your personal information.
